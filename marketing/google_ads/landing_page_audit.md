- Mobile status: Responsive templates and viewport meta exist; representative browser/curl checks should be repeated before launch after import.
- Internal funnel: `/blogs/a-coruna-through-the-lens-tower-of-hercules` -> related articles/blogs -> `/book` preview -> `/book/signed` or retailer -> newsletter/Camino.
